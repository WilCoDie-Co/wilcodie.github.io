Squiddy's Adventures

Created by Jude Gehrke (2019 – 2025)

A web-series on YouTube about a squid-like octopus named Squiddy, going on random adventures seeing the greatest parts of the world and trying to solve every problem!

About the Show

Welcome to the WilCoDie Wiki archive for Squiddy's Adventures. Spanning Episodes 1 through 5 (with the Finale coming soon!), the series follows Squiddy alongside a rotating cast of characters through snack reviews, wild holiday specials, and intense parodies.
